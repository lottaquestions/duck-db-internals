## Chapter 6 — `06-subquery-decorrelation.md`

DuckDB changes documented (new §6.11 plus inline callouts):
- `PushDownAggregate`: old join-type check replaced by `drop_missing_keys`;
  repeated empty grouping sets and `GROUPING()` handled (multiplier join).
  `lateral_empty_grouping_sets.test`.
- `MaterializeCorrelatedAggregateRewrites`: special-handling aggregate
  (`count`) as correlation key. `test_correlated_aggregate_correlation_key.test`.
- `PushDownJoin` ASOF fix (`selects_over_right`). `lateral_asof_join.test`.
- Lateral join reading a CTE no longer restarts decorrelation.
  `lateral_cte_list_correlation.test` (250 / 367).
- `error()` ignored as volatile when choosing `perform_delim`
  (`IsVolatileIgnoringError`).
- Smaller: correlated IN/ANY on a TUPLE; correlated IN under MARK join; view
  filter below SINGLE/MARK delim joins; linear-time UNION ALL of subqueries;
  `TrySwitchSingleToLeft` refuses multiple grouping sets; CTE join filter
  pusher restricts recursive partitions (optimizer, #27072).
- `Planner::CreatePlan` skips decorrelation for `LOGICAL_PLAN_STATEMENT`.
- `delim_join_as_cte` setting is now deprecated.

Book fixes (pre-existing errors, important):
- **DelimJoins → CTEs.** With the default `delim_join_as_cte = true` (already
  the default at d8a1bd4f4f), `DelimJoinCTERewriter` rewrites every DelimJoin
  into plain joins over `__duckdb_delim_*` / `__duckdb_delim_dedup_*`
  materialized CTEs right after flattening, and `VerifyNoDelim` asserts none
  remain. New §6.4.1; §6.7 and §6.10 now say the optimizer's `Deliminator`
  and the join-order DelimJoin special case only see DelimJoins when the
  setting is off. Chapters 7, 8, 9 and 19 may repeat the old claim that the
  optimizer / physical planner receive `LOGICAL_DELIM_JOIN`s by default —
  worth checking by their owners.
- Flattening runs once in `Planner::CreatePlan` after binding, not "inside the
  binder immediately after a `LogicalDependentJoin` is built" (fixed in §6.3,
  §6.7; Chapter 5 §5.7 too).
- The COUNT correctness box now explains the marker + `CASE` rewrite
  (`RewriteCorrelatedAggregates`), which is what actually yields `0`.
- `perform_delim = false` is chosen for volatile subqueries or LIST /
  STRUCT-of-LIST correlated columns, not "for UNNEST" as such.
- The paper is cited in `plan_subquery.cpp:524-525`, not in a directory
  comment; `RewriteInlineCTEDedupCandidate` lives in `UnnestRewriter`;
  `RelationManager::ExtractJoinRelations` (plural); `RECURSIVE_KEY_JOIN`
  added to the physical join list.

Citations: 6 DiDi-08 citations converted to `[@didi08, ...]`;
